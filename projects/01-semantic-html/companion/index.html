Hello neighbor,

A loose chair leg. A torn pocket. A lamp that has gone quiet. Sometimes a small repair is all it takes to keep a familiar thing in use.

We are opening the workshop for a morning of shared repairs. Bring one everyday object and a little curiosity. You do not need tools or experience to take part.

Before you come

  1. Choose one object and note what is not working.
  2. Pack any loose pieces that belong with it.
  3. Bring a bag to take everything home.

There will be places to sit and room to work at your own pace. Watching and asking questions are welcome, too.

We may not finish every repair. We can start by understanding the problem together.

Behind the page

Small choices.
Clearer reading.

This companion has its own content and stylesheet. The graded course letter remains unchanged.

For a paper copy, use your browser's Print command. The print layout keeps the invitation and removes the surrounding navigation.

Give the content a useful structure

Headings identify the invitation and its sections. A description list pairs each event detail with its label. The preparation steps use an ordered list because they describe a sequence.

Keep the reading path comfortable

The letter has a limited width and generous line spacing. At narrow widths, the introduction moves above it. Long text can wrap without making the whole page scroll sideways.

Use familiar ways to move around

The first keyboard link skips to the invitation. Links and these disclosures have visible focus indicators. Native disclosure elements provide interaction without JavaScript. Their actual keyboard behavior is checked separately from their visual appearance.
